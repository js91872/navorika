## Supporting-content opportunities within existing clusters

### Construction

- Add worked examples that connect concrete volume to cement, sand, gravel, and rebar estimates.
- Explain waste allowances consistently across flooring, tile, paint, roofing, and asphalt tools.
- Add limitations around supplier pack sizes, drawings, local codes, engineered design, and site conditions.

### Finance

- Cover monthly versus annual rate conversion, contribution timing, fees, taxes, inflation, and return uncertainty where relevant.
- Use one strong canonical route for EMI/loan-payment variants and one for recurring-investment variants; do not create pages for every synonym.
- Keep time-sensitive tax and regulated-product information tied to dated authoritative sources.

### Developer

- Describe accepted input, output, encoding, validation behavior, and security boundaries.
- Distinguish decoding/inspection from verification, encryption, authentication, or sanitization.
- Add copyable examples only where they demonstrate actual current behavior.

### Health

- Make screening versus diagnosis language prominent.
- Describe formula choice, population limitations, units, and interpretation ranges without implying individualized treatment.
- Connect energy, body-composition, activity, and heart-rate estimates as separate signals rather than a single health score.

### PDF and image

- Describe local processing only where the implementation truly remains in the browser.
- Explain destructive versus reversible operations, output format, metadata handling, quality trade-offs, and browser limitations.
- Avoid “without losing quality” unless the specific operation is lossless and verified.

## Measurement plan

For each change, record the canonical route, old and new metadata, query family, reason, and date. Review search impressions, click-through rate, engagement, and task completion after enough data accumulates. Do not create a new URL solely because a query uses a different word order or synonym.
